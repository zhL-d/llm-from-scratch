
Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree. It had many branches and leaves. One day, a little bird came to sit on the tree. The bird tried to balance on a branch, but it was hard. The bird kept trying and did not give up.
As the bird was balancing, it felt an itch on its wing. The itch was not nice, and the little bird wanted it to go away. So, the bird used its beak to scratch the itch. The itch went away, and the bird was happy.
After the itch was gone, the bird kept trying to balance on the branch. The old tree was very kind and did not mind. Soon, the little bird learned to balance very well. The bird and the old tree became good friends, and they were happy together.

Once upon a time there was a nation, full of people who tied the bad things that were happening together. Everyday people complained about how bad the nation was.
One day three wise children were walking. They were looking for something to make the nation better. Suddenly, one of them noticed a big tree. The wise child said, "Let's tie the tree to the nation. That will make the nation better!"
Another one of the wise children replied, "But it's a bad tree. It won't make the nation better like you think it will."
The other wise child said, "No, I think tying the bad tree to the nation will make us think more carefully before we do bad things. That way, we can keep the nation good."
The three wise children smiled. They knew that tying a bad tree to the nation would be their way of keeping the nation good. And from then on, whenever people did bad things, the bad tree reminded them to be more careful and keep the nation good.

The butterfly flew closer and closer and landed on the flower. Such a special place was perfect to spend the day. The butterfly decided to take a rest here, so it took a nap in the middle of the flower.
When the butterfly woke up, it saw that the flower had changed. Now it was full of wide and colourful butterflies! The butterfly looked around in amazement. It realized that all the other butterflies had come to this special place to spend the day. The butterfly was so happy that it flew up in the air and said goodbye to its new friends, the colourful butterflies. 
The butterfly flew away and found lots of other flowers to spend some time with. It was a happy butterfly and it thanked the wide, colourful flower for making it so happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to help her mom with the laundry. One day, while they were folding clothes, Sally's mom saw a persistent ant trying to climb up the table. Sally's mom said, "Sally, look at that ant! It's trying so hard to get up the table."

Her mom saw her sad face and gave her a big hug to comfort her. "It's okay, Sally. We can fold the laundry again." And just like that, the laundry was neatly folded, and Sally felt happy again.
